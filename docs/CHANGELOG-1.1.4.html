TNK PROGRESSION — 1.1.4

Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1.262 · Java 21

Boss and miniboss balance. Reduce inflated early encounter minimums, separate minibosses from full bosses and make roaming Apotheosis invaders follow player level.

Boss minimums

  • Set the Warden minimum to 35, Clawdian to 30, Kraken to 45, Magnetron to 30 and Brainiac to 20.
  • Keep all 16 End Remastered eye-boss default minimums below 60. Ignis, Harbinger, Maledictus, Leviathan, Ancient Remnant, Luxtructosaurus, Helvar and Possessed Paladin use 55.
  • Add Last of Deepslate at 55, using its verified Archaion entity ID.
  • Set the Ender Dragon minimum to 75.
  • Reduce native miniboss minimums to at most 35. Cataclysm minibosses associated with level-55 eye bosses use 25; Ender Golem and Clawdian use 30.
  • Reduce Legendary Monsters roaming miniboss minimums while retaining the full-boss classification of Possessed Paladin, Cloud Golem and Obliterator.

Roaming encounters and level caps

  • Apotheosis invaders spawn at nearest eligible player level +5, capped at 100, without random variance or native dungeon-boss minimums.
  • Apotheosis elites use ordinary nearby-player variance without inheriting native boss minimums. Preserve Apotheosis equipment, effects, stat bonuses, rewards and spawn frequency.
  • Native minibosses retain their reduced minimum or player +10 rule and cap at 100. Full bosses retain the 110 cap.
  • Keep verified Spiral arena levels and full-boss groups separate from the normal-world miniboss rules.

Saves and compatibility

  • Migrate unchanged old config defaults once. Preserve custom values and removed old entries; add the newly supported Last of Deepslate entry when missing.
  • Preserve saved enemy levels, except non-arena miniboss levels above 100 clamp at load without healing or stacking modifiers. New minimums apply to new level assignments.
  • Preserve character progress, HUD preferences, normal loot rules and explicit dungeon rewards.
  • Retain the Spiral Abyss 2.0.5/2.0.6 crash fixes from 1.1.3 and safe loading when optional mods are absent.

Installation and validation

Replace the previous Progression JAR on both clients and server, then restart. No configuration or player-data reset is required. Boss minimums remain subject to the existing player +10 scaling rule.

Validation: Java 21 build, 46 automated tests and a real NeoForge 21.1.262 dedicated-server check passed. Server checks covered player-relative encounter markers, level caps, NBT reloads, saved health, modifiers, badges and level queries. Apotheosis marker formats were simulated from inspected source; no complete modpack or graphical gameplay test was performed.
