# Towns & Kingdoms Progression — 1.1.0

A character sheet for the life you build and the adventures you take. **Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge · Java 21.**

Press **I**, or click the player portrait in your survival inventory. Earn direct character XP, choose from 20 origins, unlock 15 ascended backgrounds with a Sigil, collect titles and shape a build through the optional skill tree.
